based on official U.S. Department of Labor FY2025 filing data. If a company filed certified

[`data/h1b-sponsors-fy2025.csv`](data/h1b-sponsors-fy2025.csv), and every
company has a detail page at https://flexapply.org/sponsors/companies/.
